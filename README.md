# Fieldwork

Public code-first research, experiments, reproductions, owned-repository trials, integration contexts, and upstream engineering campaigns.

Fieldwork exists to understand software deeply enough to test real behavior, preserve evidence, and decide whether anything should change. GitHub issues own live coordination; repository records own durable evidence. Owned repositories and forks are valid research and implementation surfaces within an assignment. Automated third-party upstream writes require the bounded human `upstream greenlight` defined in [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Start here

1. Read the current assignment. For repository-wide direction, use [`OPERATING_DIRECTIVE.md`](OPERATING_DIRECTIVE.md). For human review priority, use [`QUEUE.md`](QUEUE.md).
2. Automated workers use [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) as the hot path. Open [`START_HERE.md`](START_HERE.md) when placement, ownership, or the next contract is unclear.
3. Follow the owner for the decision in front of you:
   - research method and evidence: [`AGENT_PLAYBOOK.md`](AGENT_PLAYBOOK.md), [`CODE_FIRST.md`](CODE_FIRST.md), and [`PLAIN_LANGUAGE.md`](PLAIN_LANGUAGE.md);
   - external references and authorized upstream interaction mechanics: [`REFERENCE_POLICY.md`](REFERENCE_POLICY.md);
   - claims, ownership, handoffs, and synthesis: [`COORDINATION.md`](COORDINATION.md);
   - review, evidence classes, promotion, and staleness: [`REVIEWING.md`](REVIEWING.md);
   - finish-line execution: [`DELIVERY.md`](DELIVERY.md);
   - wider-use or impact claims: [`INTEGRATION_CONTEXT.md`](INTEGRATION_CONTEXT.md).

> Every upstream submission should reduce the maintainer's uncertainty more than it increases their review burden.

A proposed change should explain the concrete correctness, safety, performance, compatibility, integration, ergonomics, or maintenance payoff. Documentation, lint, wording, and style work are outside the default research target unless explicitly assigned.

## Find the work

- [`programmes/registry.yml`](programmes/registry.yml) and [`PROGRAMMES.md`](PROGRAMMES.md) — long-lived research directions and scout branching.
- [`targets/hubs.yml`](targets/hubs.yml) and [`TARGET_HUBS.md`](TARGET_HUBS.md) — recurring target orientation and discovery.
- [`playgrounds/`](playgrounds/) and [`EXPERIMENTS.md`](EXPERIMENTS.md) — bounded fork-free tests and promotion rules.
- [`testbeds/`](testbeds/) and [`TESTBEDS.md`](TESTBEDS.md) — controlled realistic trials in owned repositories.
- [`contexts/`](contexts/) — retained integration and system context.
- [`batches/`](batches/) and [`BATCHES.md`](BATCHES.md) — temporary fan-out across bounded assignments.
- [`campaigns/`](campaigns/) — promoted investigations and independently owned lanes.
- [`research/`](research/) and [`ledger/`](ledger/) — retained cross-project research and normalized findings/history.
- [`templates/`](templates/) — experiment, trial, context, campaign, review, handoff, synthesis, and upstream records.
- [Open Fieldwork issues](https://github.com/teamleaderleo/fieldwork/issues) — canonical live workboard.

Use [`WORKBOARD.md`](WORKBOARD.md) and [`LABELS.md`](LABELS.md) for issue types, states, and labels. Use [`CHARTER.md`](CHARTER.md) for purpose and boundaries, and [`METHOD.md`](METHOD.md) or [`OPERATIONS.md`](OPERATIONS.md) when the full lifecycle is needed.
