Research programmes connect findings across targets and campaigns. They ask questions larger than one patch while keeping individual experiments bounded.

## Research standard

A programme should define:

- the question;
- operational definitions;
- evidence sources;
- ethical and privacy boundaries;
- competing explanations;
- limits on generalisation;
- useful outputs independent of a preferred conclusion.

Programmes must not use upstream communities as involuntary experimental subjects. Observe public process carefully, avoid manipulative submissions, and do not create low-value work merely to measure reactions.
