# Campaign 0001: Proof-Carrying Contributions

State: `observed`

## Question

Can a heavily AI-assisted developer produce upstream contributions that measurably lower maintainer verification burden while preserving clear human accountability?

## Motivation

Code generation is cheap. Establishing that a change is necessary, correct, compatible, secure, and maintainable remains expensive. Fieldwork needs an operating method that increases investigative throughput without exporting uncertainty to upstream projects.

## Scope

This campaign will develop and test a reusable upstream packet across genuine engineering problems encountered in our work. It will compare the usefulness of reproductions, regression tests, causal explanations, alternatives, and recovery information.

## Non-goals

- manufacturing submissions for sample size;
- optimising for merge rate or response speed;
- judging maintainers for declining work;
- proving that AI-generated contributions are categorically good or bad;
- automating upstream contact.

## Stop conditions

- the method becomes heavier than the changes it supports;
- useful evaluation would require manipulative or low-value submissions;
- we cannot distinguish packet quality from project-specific governance;
- the campaign ceases to improve work we already value.

## Expected outputs

- an upstream packet template;
- campaign closeout conventions;
- examples of useful and excessive evidence;
- a small set of genuine case studies;
- negative results and revised hypotheses;
- possible tooling for validating evidence bundles.
