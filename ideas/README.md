# Fieldwork ideas

This directory is for durable product and engineering ideas that are useful enough to preserve but have not yet become a campaign, accepted design, or target-project proposal.

## What belongs here

Use `ideas/` for:

- product hypotheses that cut across repositories;
- design precedents worth remembering;
- small executable models that test an idea before a real implementation exists;
- ergonomics notes and interface sketches;
- failure-pattern writeups that may become reusable project policy;
- deliberately opinionated “if we owned the product” explorations.

An idea may later graduate into an experiment, campaign, ADR, project issue, implementation branch, or nothing at all. Graduation should be explicit. Until then, material here is exploratory and carries no authority over target repositories.

## Evidence labels

Keep claims distinguishable:

- **observed** — read directly from source, execution, or retained provider evidence;
- **model-executed** — demonstrated in a toy or synthetic model;
- **inferred** — a reasoned consequence of observed behavior;
- **preference** — a product/design choice we would make;
- **open** — deliberately unresolved.
